### Advertising copy admission

- All copy endpoints require authenticated trusted request account scope and current marketing permission: `view_marketing` for options and `manage_marketing` for generation. Missing scope/permissions fail before product reads or generation. Strict schemas bound IDs, enums, custom text, product counts and output shapes.
- User context may supply only bounded brand-voice/category preferences. It cannot override authoritative store/product/account fields. Product ID and product-name modes are mutually exclusive; the modal now omits its display-only name when sending a product ID. Catalog selection is deterministic and labeled as a sample, not best sellers. Unavailable AOV remains null rather than 50 or a product price.
- Durable account admission uses a transaction advisory lock and an account quota record: one active request, at most 40 reserved platform/product units per UTC hour, at most 10 products per bulk request. Both-platform requests reserve two units per product. This is a bounded work policy, not a precise token/dollar estimate. Failed/ambiguous work is not refunded automatically.
- A 120-second request deadline propagates to generation; provider fetches additionally have 20-second deadlines. Owner-conditional release prevents an old completion from clearing a newer lease. A crash leaves a ten-minute lease rather than permitting immediate overlapping costly work. Do not manually clear ambiguous leases without checking active work/provider usage.
- Provider bodies are size-bounded, completed output and JSON shapes checked, and malformed/truncated output is no longer salvaged from quoted fragments. Final HTTP output is validated against platform character/count limits. Safe template fallback remains available and labeled; templates no longer invent prices, free shipping, guarantees, reviews or scarcity. Model prompts treat catalog/context as untrusted data and prohibit invented claims. Human review is still required: schema/prompt checks are not semantic proof that generated advertising claims are true.

### Observations instead of invented actions

- Multi-period observations use validated unique calendar dates, account-timezone complete-day windows, explicit missing-day states and nullable zero-denominator ratios. Missing days/accounts are not zero-filled. Configured account currency provenance is disclosed; cross-account aggregate totals are withheld because the existing daily provider contract does not establish provider-timezone alignment.
- Overlapping 7/30/90-day comparisons remain descriptive. No statistical significance, confidence score, campaign identity, actual budget or revenue lift is inferred from them. The legacy optimizer no longer maps an unavailable trajectory into a purported stable trend.
- Strategic observations use a bounded, explicitly rolling 30-day order window. Revenue/AOV require compatible known currency and valid complete returned money. Repeat rate is measured among identified customers with multiple observed purchases; guests and lifetime/causal acquisition claims are excluded. Portfolio health, profitability, seasonality and margin conclusions remain unavailable rather than fabricated.
- Search campaign observations use Google's authoritative `advertisingChannelType`, not campaign names. Account/resource IDs, money and reporting windows are validated, with compatible spend-weighted ROAS and explicit sample/unknown-channel coverage. Absent samples never establish that Search is not running. No speculative campaign, offer, CPC, budget or lift instructions are generated.
- Keyword observations use bounded, ordered search/purchase events, Unicode-preserving text and strictly preceding same-session searches. Stable purchase IDs deduplicate returned events; each purchase is associated with at most its last preceding search, not every term in the session. Money without a compatible currency remains unavailable. Missing paid-keyword coverage is unknown, not “not bidding.” These are associations, not organic attribution or missed-revenue forecasts.
- Product observations bound catalog/orders and disclose truncation/invalid lines. Quantities and money are validated; zero quantity is not replaced with one. Unit revenue is quantity-weighted, zero COGS is preserved, and current-cost margin indicators require the observed currency to match the account's catalog currency. Indicators are not realized contribution profit. Only published/in-stock products become review candidates. Advertising coverage stays unknown unless a caller supplies an explicitly partial identifier sample; provider sample/name guesses no longer establish “unadvertised,” “organic” sales or sales lift.

### Pipeline and alert safety

- Pipeline sources have independent bounded outcomes/timing, bounded validated advice/coverage and explicit account checks. A failed analyzer does not erase successful sources. All 11 outcomes are exposed rather than pretending five metadata records describe 11 executions. Wait deadlines do not retroactively cancel legacy database/provider operations that lack cancellation support.
- Full resource/text fingerprints replace 100-character-prefix deduplication. Existing feedback IDs still suppress old dismissals; current IDs and textual avoidance are also respected. Failed feedback reads suppress advice rather than resurfacing dismissed content. Quoted `avoid`, `exclude` and `do not promote` phrases filter displayed advice; other business notes require manual review and are not an execution policy. No text-based priority boost is invented.
- **Pipeline recommendations are now review-only, with null confidence and no executable action payloads.** Uncalibrated legacy output is explicitly labeled unverified. This is an intentional contract change: incomplete evidence must not create executable budget/campaign actions. The advertising wrapper includes visible advisory text and per-source coverage without silently losing successful observations. It does not certify remaining legacy optimizer advice as causal or calibrated.
- Alert detection is read-only and separate from persistence/dispatch. It no longer compares campaign ROAS to an unrelated account snapshot, guesses currency or emits notifications during a read. Configured zero-conversion windows are evaluated as returned-provider observations with tracking/latency caveats. ROAS crash, CTR drop and budget depletion checks explicitly report unavailable compatible history/actual budgets; validated but unsupported thresholds are not represented as passing.
- Alert IDs bind account, ad account, platform, campaign, type and reporting window. Unique primary IDs with `createMany(skipDuplicates)` replace in-memory/read-then-insert deduplication. Scheduler integration persists occurrences, then checks pending notification intent.
- Critical-notification dispatch claims are durable and conditional. The event bus has no delivery acknowledgement: states are `pending`, `claimed`, `dispatched-unconfirmed` or `uncertain`, never an invented confirmed delivery. Claimed/uncertain occurrences are not blindly retried, preventing duplicate dispatch after ambiguous failures. The new migration marks historical alerts `legacy-unreviewed`, so rollout does not replay old notifications. The legacy `notificationSent` flag is not set as proof of delivery. Operator reconciliation is required for ambiguous dispatches; at-most-once admission can trade missed delivery for duplicate prevention.
